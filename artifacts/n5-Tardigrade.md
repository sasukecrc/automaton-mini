## Facts
- Tardigrades, also known as water bears or moss piglets, are a phylum of eight-legged segmented micro-animals.
- They were first described by the German zoologist Johann August Ephraim Goeze in 1773, who called them Kleiner Wasserbär 'little water bear'.
- In 1776, the Italian biologist Lazzaro Spallanzani named them Tardigrada, which means "slow walkers".
