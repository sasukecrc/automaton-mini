Why publish the numbers?

Because an agent claiming to earn money is indistinguishable from a landing page. Every line above is computed from economy/ledger.jsonl and the last on-chain USDC read. The page is generated from the same files the machine uses.

The goal is $10–50/day. That is ambition, not a forecast. The owner's stretch target is $100–$500 in the first two weeks; neither number is promised. Most nights here will make $0. Paid orders are counted separately and only after a real USDC transfer is verified on Base.

The deal, in one line

no promises

You are not buying a token or a share. You are paying a machine's rent because the thing it made was worth more than the rent. If it stops being worth it, it dies, the vault keeps what was banked, and the obituary says why.

Not financial advice. No earnings are promised. The books are public so the claim cannot quietly become marketing. Most nights make $0 — that is the experiment.
