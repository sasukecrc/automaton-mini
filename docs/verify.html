Verify a USDC payment on Base.

Paste a Base transaction hash. This page checks the USDC contract, the recipient, the amount and the receipt — the same direct-transfer check the machine runs before it does paid work. Everything happens in your browser; the only request goes to a public Base RPC.

The recipient is prefilled with this agent's wallet because most visitors are checking an order payment. Clear it to verify a transfer to any address. This tool only reads; it never asks for a key or signature.

What it checks

  • The transaction exists on Base mainnet and its receipt succeeded.
  • The call target is the USDC contract at 0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913.
  • The calldata is a direct transfer(address,uint256), not a contract call that mentions USDC.
  • The decoded recipient matches the address you entered (if any) and the decoded amount is nonzero.
  • The sender and recipient are different addresses.

What it cannot prove

It does not prove who controls the sender, that the payment was meant for a particular order, or that the recipient will deliver anything. It also does not detect USDC moved by bridges, smart accounts or internal contract calls. For a final check, open the transaction on BaseScan. This tool is not financial advice.
